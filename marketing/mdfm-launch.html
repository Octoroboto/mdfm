The music

Never the same song twice

  • Generated live: boom bap, g funk, lo fi, break beats, trap and more.
  • Real song forms: intros, verses, hooks, breaks and bridges, with key changes and fills.
  • Next Song skips ahead. Station Break plays a spot now.
  • Let It Ride keeps each song going about two minutes. Underwater makes it muffled and drifting.
  • Now playing shows the title, style and BPM on the screen.

The receiver

Knobs you actually turn

  • LOW, MID, HIGH tone knobs and a big VOLUME knob.
  • LED equalizer and twin VU needle meters with peak lights.
  • RED, GREEN, BLUE knobs recolor the lights. AUTO cycles them.
  • Flies land on the screen. Grab the bug spray.
  • Captions of every spoken line, full keyboard control, and screen-reader labels.

Tune in

Three steps to the beat

  1. Download MDFM Radio from the releases page. Pick arm64 for an Apple chip Mac, the other file for Intel.

  2. Open it and drag MDFM Radio to Applications. The first time, macOS may ask: go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

  3. Press SOUND. Pick a skin, turn up the bass, and spray a fly.

Updates show up as a lit badge on the receiver. They never install themselves.
