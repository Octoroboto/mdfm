The music

Never the same song twice

  • Generated live: trap, boom bap, neo-soul, amapiano and more, now with gothic dance pop, alt and emo dance pop, pop ballads, and four-minute DJ sets full of vocal chops.
  • Sung leads from a synth voice, distorted guitars for the emo tracks, and in the app, real words chopped into the DJ sets.
  • Real song forms: intros, verses, hooks, breaks and bridges, with key changes and fills.
  • Next Song skips ahead. Station Break plays a spot now.
  • Let It Ride keeps each song going about two minutes. Underwater makes it muffled and drifting.
  • Now playing shows the title, style and BPM on the screen.

The receiver

Knobs you actually turn

  • LOW, MID, HIGH tone knobs and a big VOLUME knob.
  • LED equalizer and twin VU needle meters with peak lights.
  • RED, GREEN, BLUE knobs recolor the lights. AUTO cycles them.
  • Flies land on the screen. Grab the bug spray.
  • ACCESSIBILITY drawer (Cmd+Option+A): text size, contrast, calmer motion, read aloud, reading fonts, and captions of every voice and song.
  • Full keyboard control and screen-reader labels, plus text versions of the EQ and meters.

Skins

Make it yours

Pick a look from the Skin dropdown in Settings. New skins arrive by themselves, no update needed. Some hide things to tap: menus that open, spoof pages, and cupboards that swing open.

Tune in

Three steps to the beat

  1. Download MDFM Radio: Apple chip Mac or Intel Mac. Not sure? Apple menu, About This Mac: it says Chip for Apple chip, Processor for Intel.

  2. Open it and drag MDFM Radio to Applications. The first time, macOS may ask: go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

  3. Press SOUND. Pick a skin, turn up the bass, and spray a fly.

Updates show up as a lit badge on the receiver. They never install themselves.
