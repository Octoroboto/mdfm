MD HubMEDIA DEVELOPMENT

The Automation Hub, out of the browser tab and onto your desk.

The Media Development Hub in its own steel frame, with MDFM 99.9 HD built in and fed live local weather, sports and company news. Free for Mac, for the RentVision team.

The idea

Same hub. Its own box.

MD Hub opens the live hub from claude.ai, so it's always the current version: post ideas and problems, check what's open, follow up. The app adds the frame, the live data and the safety lights.

What you get

A receiver that happens to run the hub.

No grey bar

Just steel.

No macOS title bar. The window controls are on the steel itself: POWER, MIN and FULL. Drag it by the metal, or double-click the strip to zoom.

Always current

Always live.

The hub page loads from claude.ai each time, so new categories and fixes show up without an app update.

MDFM 99.9 HD, the full radio

Everything MDFM Radio has, right in the hub.

VU meters and the LED EQ, the light knobs, VOLUME and LOW / MID / HIGH, LET IT RIDE and UNDERWATER, and a Skin dropdown. In the app, MDFM gets live weather and sports for where you are, Slack company news on the hour, and real voices that the EQ shapes too.

You choose

Same switches, two places.

Turn off station IDs, ads, call-ins, plays or anything else, on the receiver or in Settings. They stay in step, and match MDFM Radio word for word. So does your skin.

Skins

Change the whole look.

Pick a skin in Settings and the steel frame and the hub's receiver change together. Leave it on "House default" and you'll get whatever look MedDev picks next. Skins can be anything, from a saloon of wood and rusty nails to something that drips, and new ones arrive by themselves. The dials, glass and lights always work the same.

Settings

One button, everything in it.

Your skin, what plays between songs, city and units for weather and sports, Slack news, what's out of date, and both Bouncer logs. Reload the hub is there too (or ⌘R).

Peace of mind, in plain sight

Two lights. Always on.

The Bouncer and Futureproofer badges sit on the steel strip all the time. Green means all good, amber means something to look at, and red blinks. Click either one for the details.

Bouncer

The app only reads.

Every request the app itself makes is checked against a locked rule list before it leaves your Mac: weather, location, scoreboards, Slack (read-only) and its own releases. Everything else is blocked. The hub page on claude.ai is watched and anything unusual is logged, without breaking Claude.

Futureproofer

Asks. Never forces.

When a new MD Hub comes out, it asks: Download, Later or Skip this version. It also keeps an eye on the app's engine, known security problems and your Mac. New looks and skins arrive quietly and show on the next launch. Nothing installs on its own.

Install

On your desk in about two minutes.

No Terminal. Pick the download that matches your Mac. Not sure which? Apple menu → About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple silicon.

Apple siliconM1 and newer · downloads the newest version IntelOlder Macs · downloads the newest version

Each button gets the newest MD Hub. Release notes are on the mdfm releases page.

Why the extra step? MD Hub is shared directly by MedDev, not through the App Store, and it isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet. So macOS asks you to allow it once (step 4). After that it opens like any other app.

  1. Open the download.

    Double-click the .dmg. A window opens with MD Hub and an Applications folder.

  2. Drag it into Applications.

    Drag the MD Hub icon onto the Applications folder. You can eject the .dmg after.

  3. Open it.

    Find MD Hub in Applications or Spotlight. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app. Click Done.

  4. Allow it once.

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, and click Open Anyway next to MD Hub. Enter your Mac password if asked. Only needed once.

  5. Sign in to Claude.

    Use your RentVision Claude account. If Google sign-in won't finish inside the app, choose Continue with email and enter the code Claude emails you.

  6. Allow location, or set a city.

    For local weather and sports. Prefer not to share, or it can't find you? Click SETTINGS on the strip and type a city.

  7. Tick the speaker.

    MDFM is on the air. Pick a skin in SETTINGS if you like. Both badges should be green. When there's a new MD Hub, the app asks before doing anything.
